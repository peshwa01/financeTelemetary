Privacy policy

Finance Telemetry, by Skyvane Labs · Last updated 19 October 2026

The short version

Finance Telemetry keeps your financial information on your iPhone. Skyvane Labs does not run servers for the app, does not collect your data, and cannot see it.

What the app stores, and where

Account balances, transactions, fixed deposits, PPF entries, investment holdings, family member names you add, and the passwords you choose to remember for statement PDFs. All of it is stored only on your iPhone: the data file is encrypted with AES-256 using a key kept in the iPhone's Keychain (this device only), and is further protected by iOS Data Protection and the app's Face ID / passcode lock. Statement PDFs are read in memory and not kept.

Google user data (Gmail)

Connecting Gmail is optional. If you connect it, the app requests the read-only Gmail scope (gmail.readonly) and uses it only to:

  • search your mailbox for emails with PDF statements from financial institutions (such as NSDL, CDSL, CAMS, KFintech and banks) or with "statement" in the subject;
  • download those PDF attachments directly to your iPhone, read the figures in them, and discard the files;
  • read your email address, to label which family member the mailbox belongs to.

Your Google access tokens are stored only in your iPhone's Keychain. Requests go directly from your iPhone to Google. Gmail data is never sent to Skyvane Labs or any other party, is not used for advertising, is not sold, is not used to train any AI or machine learning model, and is not read by any person. The app never sends, deletes or modifies email.

Finance Telemetry's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

We do not share, sell or transfer your data. Nothing leaves your phone except the requests your phone makes to Google (if you connect Gmail) and to public sources of fund prices and exchange rates, which contain no personal information.

Analytics and tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. Apple may provide anonymous crash reports if you have opted in on your device.

Your control

  • Disconnect a Gmail account in the app (Settings › Gmail), or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete everything with Settings › Delete all data, or by deleting the app.
  • Because data never leaves your device, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

The app is intended for adults managing their family's finances.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
