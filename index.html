Finance Telemetry

Financial data monitoring for your family, by Skyvane Labs.

Finance Telemetry is an iPhone app that brings a family's bank balances, fixed deposits, PPF, mutual funds, shares and recurring bills into one private snapshot.

Your data stays on your phone. Statements are read on the device and discarded. Everything the app keeps is encrypted on the iPhone and locked with Face ID. There are no Skyvane Labs servers and no accounts to create.

If you connect Gmail, the app uses read-only access to find statement emails from your banks and depositories (for example NSDL, CDSL, CAMS, KFintech and your bank), and downloads those PDFs straight to your phone. It never sends, deletes or changes email. Read the privacy policy for details.
